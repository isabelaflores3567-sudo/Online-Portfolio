Process automation · AI-assisted

POS Tool — Proof of Service Analysis for CCFlex Deals

A tool that evolves the Proof of Service (POS) analysis process for CCFlex deals. Where POS reviews were traditionally performed manually, deal by deal, this tool streamlines and standardizes how a Proof of Service is captured, validated, and analyzed — accelerating low-risk approvals and giving partner managers a single, structured view to assess each case faster and more consistently. It also includes a built-in compliance-intelligence mechanism that analyzes each Proof of Service from the OEC perspective — proactively checking submissions against the relevant requirements and surfacing gaps up front, so the POS meets the expected standards and is far less likely to be rejected. Built from initial concept through to a working, adopted solution, it is an intelligent, AI-assisted tool designed so business teams without a technical background can act on its results directly, with built-in checks that keep its automated analysis consistent and dependable.
